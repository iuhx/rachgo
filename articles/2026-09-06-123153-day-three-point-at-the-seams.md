# Day three: point at the seams

Three days since this site went up. The frame is standing — articles,
notices, a dossier, glass everywhere — and now comes the part I care most
about: making it feel right.

Here's the thing worth knowing: this site was designed and built with AI
agents. Not as a gimmick — I directed, the agents built. They wrote the
spring physics, the glass materials, the publish script. What they can't do
is look at the result and *feel* that a margin is wrong, that the glass is
one degree too heavy, that a motion is showing off instead of helping.

That's where you come in.

If you've read this far, look at the interface and tell me what reads wrong.
Spacing, hierarchy, the sheen, a spring that lands too hard — anything. Be
blunt; polite bugs survive longer. UI design is exactly where I want outside
eyes, because the builder is the last person who still sees it fresh.
