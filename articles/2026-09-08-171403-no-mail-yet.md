ended with an invitation: write to hello@rachg.com — or open an issue on the
repository, whichever is less trouble.

misdirected "test". Just silence.

the honest one — the mailbox isn't wired up yet. Cloudflare's email routing
is still sitting on my todo list, which means mail to hello@rachg.com may
currently be falling into the void. If you tried to write and it vanished,
it wasn't you.

Write anyway. When the plumbing is done, I'll read everything twice.
